# Aircury Connector

**Aircury Connector** is a powerful, serverless ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) tool designed to simplify data flows by seamlessly transferring datasets from various sources into your environment with minimal setup and no development effort.

I contributed to the **design and development** of the Connector landing page, focusing on creating an intuitive user interface. The platform enables users to:

- **Fetch** data from any source database, flat file, or API.
- **Automate** transformations on the source dataset.
- **Create** a relational database with an appropriate schema in your environment.
- **Schedule** data transfers based on a user-defined timetable.
- **Store** data in tables tailored to your product's specifications.

The Connector integrates with a wide range of systems, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Bromcom, ScholarPack, Pellcomp PICS, and Wonde, providing flexibility to work with any product database that offers data access.

The Aircury Connector is highly adaptable, making it an ideal solution for businesses seeking to streamline their data integration processes without the need for extensive development resources.
