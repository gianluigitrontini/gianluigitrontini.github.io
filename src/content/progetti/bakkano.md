# Bakkano

![Bakkano Landing Page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gianluigitrontini/preview-images/main/bakkano/bakkano-capture-for-github.png)

**Pinsa Products Landing Page** is a dedicated platform designed to showcase fresh Pinsa products to B2B clients, particularly food businesses, and help the company communicate the value of its offerings. The goal of this landing page is twofold: to highlight the quality and freshness of the products, and to provide an intuitive, easy-to-navigate interface for potential business clients to explore and make inquiries.

The page includes:

- **Product Showcase**: A clear, visually appealing display of all available Pinsa products, with high-quality images and concise descriptions, helping clients quickly understand the offerings.
- **B2B Value Proposition**: Sections that communicate the benefits of working with the company, including freshness, sourcing quality, and reliability for business partners.
- **Contact & Inquiry Form**: A simple and effective way for potential clients to get in touch, request more information, or place initial orders.

I handled the **design implementation and development**, ensuring a responsive layout, fast loading times, and a user-friendly experience. The visual style emphasizes the artisanal quality of the products while maintaining a professional tone suitable for a B2B audience.

This landing page serves as a bridge between the company and its professional clients, presenting products in a clear and engaging way, while supporting the company’s sales and marketing efforts.
