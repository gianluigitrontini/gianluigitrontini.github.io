# Danilo Barzaghini's multi-language Personal Website

![Danilo Barzaghini Website](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gianluigitrontini/preview-images/main/danilo-barzaghini-screenshot.png)

**Fiverr Writer Landing Page** is a simple and elegant single–page website developed to showcase the profile and services of a freelance writer. The design was provided by the client, and I focused on translating it into a responsive, functional, and performance–optimized landing page.

The page highlights key information such as biography, writing samples, and contact details, with a clean layout that keeps the content in focus. My contribution was ensuring pixel–perfect implementation, smooth responsiveness across devices, and a structure that can be easily extended if the client wishes to scale their online presence in the future.
