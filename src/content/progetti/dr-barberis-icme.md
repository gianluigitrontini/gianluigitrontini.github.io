# Dr. Barberis & ICME Studio of Cosmetic Medicine in Milan and Turin

**Cosmetic Surgeon & Aesthetic Studio Websites** are two elegant WordPress websites developed for a cosmetic surgeon and his aesthetic medicine studio, located in Milan and Turin. The client provided designs with a clear brief requesting sophistication and elegance, and I implemented them into fully functional, responsive websites.

The first website presents the surgeon’s profile, services, and patient resources, offering a professional and reassuring experience for visitors. The second website focuses on the studio, highlighting its treatments, team, and facilities, maintaining a consistent elegant aesthetic throughout.

I handled the WordPress development, ensuring pixel-perfect fidelity to the provided designs, smooth responsiveness across devices, optimized performance, and a flexible structure for future updates or expansions. The result is a cohesive, polished online presence that reflects the premium nature of the services offered.
