# Formazione ECM

**Formazione ECM Apps** are mobile applications designed for medical professionals to manage and access continuing medical education (ECM) programs. They are actively used in multiple Italian regions, including Veneto, Piemonte, Sardegna, Valle d'Aosta, and the province of Trento and Bolzano, with a solid and growing user base.

I took the lead on **refactoring the codebase**, removing numerous bugs, and improving overall performance and stability. In addition, I implemented a **redesign focused on user experience**, making the apps more intuitive, responsive, and accessible for busy medical professionals.

The apps provide essential features such as course tracking, notifications for upcoming deadlines, personalized dashboards, and seamless integration with regional healthcare systems. By enhancing reliability and usability, the apps have become a trusted tool for healthcare workers to manage their training efficiently. Client feedback has been overwhelmingly positive, highlighting both the improved UX and the smooth performance across different devices.

Developed with NBS, these apps combine technical robustness with a practical, user-centered approach, supporting professionals in meeting ECM requirements while simplifying administrative tasks.
