Smart4Care, Smart4Med, and Smart4Hospital are healthcare applications designed for medical professionals to improve communication, organization, and data management between patients, doctors, and medical facilities.

#### Smart4Care

Smart4Care enhances the dialogue between patients and healthcare providers, offering easy access to information, services, and clinical documentation.

#### Smart4Med

Smart4Med is tailored for healthcare professionals, providing tools to optimize patient management and support daily clinical activities.

#### Smart4Hospital

Smart4Hospital focuses on the digitalization of hospital processes, making organizational and administrative management more efficient.
