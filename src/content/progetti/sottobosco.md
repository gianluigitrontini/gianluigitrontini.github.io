# Sottobosco

**Sottobosco** is a digital platform that connects consumers with local and organic producers, promoting sustainable agriculture and conscious consumption. Beyond e-commerce, the platform also offers educational initiatives, providing courses and resources on nutrition, environmental awareness, and sustainable practices.

As a full-stack developer, I contributed to both the **design and development** of the platform, creating a user-friendly interface and scalable architecture. Features include a marketplace for local products, vendor dashboards, and a content management system for educational materials. The platform is designed to allow future expansion, including tools for schools and agricultural training programs.

The goal of Sottobosco is to create a seamless connection between producers and consumers while raising awareness about sustainability. By combining technology with a socially-conscious mission, the platform supports small businesses, encourages responsible purchasing, and provides educational opportunities for a wider audience.
